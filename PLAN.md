## Summary

Openly-licensed, plain-language agronomy guides that summarize established good practice from authoritative sources for smallholder farmers and extension workers.

## Scope

In scope: general educational guides on established agronomy practice. Out of scope: farm-specific prescriptions, pesticide dosing, or region-specific regulatory advice.

## Risk

Medium: built on openly-licensed sources with recorded provenance; educational/derivative in nature, no personal data, and no farm-/case-specific prescriptive advice. Each deliverable must cite sources and stay within established, verifiable material.

## Approach

Each task writes one guide: why it matters, established practice, common mistakes, and sources, staying general and educational.

## Definition of shipped

Openly-licensed, plain-language agronomy guides summarizing established practice, source-cited, and handed to a farmer-support organization.
